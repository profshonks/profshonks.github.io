I am an Assistant Professor of Manufacturing (in the School of Aerospace, Industrial, Materials, and Mechanical Engineering) at the University of Nevada, Reno.

My research focuses on improving manufactured part quality through data-driven methods. I am particularly interested in developing data-efficient machine learning approaches to accelerate additive manufacturing part quality prediction and the design for additive manufacturing cycle.

Research interests
- Geometric shape representations for manufacturability analysis and surrogate models
- Machine learning surrogate models for additive manufacturability analysis, including laser powder bed fusion distortion and residual stress
- CAD/FEA dataset creation & analysis of data 
- Composite design optimization and part evaluation
- Editable CAD from text and images
- Design for additive manufacturing & additively enhanced casting
